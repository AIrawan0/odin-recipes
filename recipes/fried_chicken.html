What is Fried Chicken

Fried Chicken is a popular dish made of chicken pieces coated in seasoned flour, batter, or spices, and then pan-fried, deep-fried, pressure-fried, or air-fried.

  • A Whole chicken
  • Buttermilk
  • All purpose flour
  • Paprika
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Step by Step Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken pieces dry with paper towels. Season both sides evenly with the salt and black pepper.
  2. Dip each chicken piece into the buttermilk so it is thoroughly moistened, which helps the flour stick and tenderizes the meat.
  3. In a large shallow bowl or a sealable plastic bag, combine the all-purpose flour and paprika. Place the buttermilk-coated chicken into the flour mixture, pressing or shaking until every piece is completely coated on all sides. Let it rest on a plate for 5 minutes.
  4. Heat the vegetable or canola oil in a large, heavy-based skillet or deep pot over medium-high heat (around 350°F to 375°F). Carefully place the chicken pieces into the hot oil in batches so you do not overcrowd the pan. Fry for about 15 to 20 minutes, turning occasionally until the skin is deep golden-brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  5. Transfer the fried chicken to a wire cooling rack set over a baking sheet or paper towels to drain excess oil. Serve warm.
