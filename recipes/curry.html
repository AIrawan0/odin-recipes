What is Curry

Curry is a broad term for a dish featuring meat, fish, vegetables, or legumes cooked in a spiced sauce, gravy, or wet/dry spice paste.

  • 500g chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2–3 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • ½ cup chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp oil (coconut oil or vegetable oil)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro) to garnish (optional)

Step by Step Instructions

  1. Sauté the base: Heat the oil in a large pan or pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 30 more seconds.
  2. Toast the spices: Stir in the curry powder and cook for about 15 to 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Coat the chicken: Stir in the tomato paste, then add the chicken pieces. Toss well so the chicken is fully coated in the spice mixture.
  4. Simmer the sauce: Pour in the chicken stock and coconut milk, and season with salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle boil, then turn the heat down to low.
  5. Cook through: Let it simmer uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through, and the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Serve: Remove from heat, let it rest for 5 minutes, and garnish with fresh coriander. Serve hot over basmati rice or with warm naan.
